LPL/FINRA Compliance Maintained

Every element of the redesign maintains full regulatory compliance:

✓ BrokerCheck link prominently displayed (FINRA Rule 2210(d)(8))
✓ LPL Financial / FINRA / SIPC disclosure in footer
✓ No performance guarantees or absolute claims
✓ Educational framing throughout (not investment advice)
✓ Fixed annuity risk disclosure included
✓ Variable annuity risk disclosure included
✓ State registration / business limitation language
✓ Form CRS and Privacy Policy links in footer
✓ No testimonials (would require additional compliance review)
✓ Fair, balanced, not misleading (FINRA Rule 2210 standard)
